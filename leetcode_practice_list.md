# LeetCode Practice List — Arrays, Strings, Greedy

These are picked to avoid overlap with what you already have solved in your zip
(Two Sum, Kadane's, Rotate Array, Best Time to Buy/Sell Stock, Longest Substring
Without Repeating Characters, Anagrams, Jump Game 1 and 2, Merge Intervals, Insert
Interval, Non Overlapping Intervals, Gas Station type greedy problems, Assign
Cookies, Candy type problems via lemonade change, Can Place Flowers, Job Sequencing,
Fractional Knapsack, N Meeting In One Room, Min Platforms, Two City Scheduling,
Broken Calculator, Water Bottles, Max Units on Truck, Bag of Tokens, Split String
in Balanced Strings, Maximize Sum After K Negations, Duplicate Zeros, Remove
Duplicates, Contains Duplicate, Find All Duplicates, Palindromic Substrings,
Longest Palindromic Substring, and the various stock span or monotonic stack
problems).

## Suggested Order

Start with Arrays 1 through 10, then Strings 1 through 10, then Greedy 1 through 6.
Once those feel comfortable, move to the remaining items in each section. Container
With Most Water, 3Sum, and Subarray Sum Equals K are good array checkpoints. Gas
Station and Task Scheduler are good greedy checkpoints since they show up often in
interviews.
